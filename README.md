## 🌊 What deep daiv. is

A student AI academic club organised in domain tracks (e.g. Transformer NLP, Medical AI, recommender systems, multimodal). A track starts with paper reviews and moves to a team project presented at the club's seminar; Jiheon kept *WIL* (What I Learned) study notes along the way. The **Contents Team** publishes the club's AI magazine and the weekly e-mail newsletter *위클리 딥 다이브*.

## ✨ Highlights

- **Team Lead four times** — NLP, Medical AI, RecSys and Multimodal teams (team sizes from the archives: 4 for the persona chatbot, 3 for BU-Net).
- **From club project to publication** — the Multimodal team's video temporal grounding work was **accepted at the 2025 IEIE Summer Annual Conference** (authors: Jiheon Kang, Suyong Kim, Harim Noh, Heejae Yang).
- **Explaining research to everyone** — 3 magazine pieces and 9 newsletter issues as 에디터 져니, several tied to his own research (hallucination, video grounding, recommenders).
